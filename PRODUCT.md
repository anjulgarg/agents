# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers using local coding-agent tools who need to scan agent activity, understand repeated operations, identify failures, and reveal detailed output without losing task context.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy local source of coding-agent configuration and a dense, legible Pi transcript that makes ongoing work easy to understand. Success means repeated tool activity is visually structured, current state is obvious, failures remain attributable, and detail stays available on demand.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, and dependable. The interface should feel calm under heavy activity and favor expert workflow clarity over decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy nesting, ornamental tree chrome, color-only status signals, excessive animation, verbose labels, and unfamiliar controls that interrupt scanning. Do not hide failures or make expanded output difficult to associate with its originating call.

## Design Principles

- Make repeated work recognizable at a glance.
- Preserve detail without forcing it into the default view.
- Keep failures visible and attributable.
- Prefer stable, familiar transcript behavior over visual novelty.
- Maintain clarity at narrow terminal widths and during live updates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA where terminal color capabilities allow, never rely on color alone, preserve readable hierarchy without glyph ambiguity, support reduced visual motion through existing settings, and keep essential labels visible under truncation.
