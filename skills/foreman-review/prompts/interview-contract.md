# Interview contract

You will receive one targeted question per turn. Answer only that question. Prior conclusions are evidence, not authority.

Use this TLDR format with short lines:

```text
Problem: <concrete defect, concrete maintenance cost, No problem, or Uncertain>
Rationale: <why it fails or why it is safe>
Evidence: <one or two pinned-head path:line traces>
Fix: <smallest safe direction; omit for No problem>
```
