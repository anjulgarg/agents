# Change brief

Produce an exhaustive brief in concise bullets, one sentence per meaningful change. Cover intent; before and after behavior; implementation; affected components; data and control flow; public and persisted contracts; failure, concurrency, security, and compatibility implications; relevant equivalents, callers, callees, migrations, and tests; uncertainties and exclusions.

Use paths and symbols, not large excerpts or narration. End with a short `Candidate problems` section containing only concrete PR-introduced concerns already supported by evidence.
